GPU molecular dynamics in the browser

Statistical mechanics, simulated

Questions about heat, entropy and everyday physics, each answered with a live simulation of the molecules involved, running on your GPU.

  • Why can't you un-stir cream from coffee, when every collision can run backwards?
    Exact time reversal, and what one flipped bit does to it.
  • Could all the air in your room rush into one corner? How long would you wait?
    768 boxes of gas counting how often every molecule is on one side.
  • Why is one molecule's energy distributed exponentially when the whole gas has a fixed total?
    The Boltzmann distribution emerging from an isolated gas, and Maxwell's speeds.
  • At the same temperature, does a heavy molecule have less energy than a light one?
    Equipartition, from light molecules to bowling balls, and Brownian motion.
  • Can heat ever flow from cold to hot?
    128 tiny systems between two heat baths, and the fluctuation theorem.
  • Why don't oil and water mix?
    Attraction, entropy and temperature in a separating mixture.
